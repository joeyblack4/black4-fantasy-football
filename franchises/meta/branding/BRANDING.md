## Name
**Meta Mesh**

The mesh is a real football concept — two crossing routes that read the coverage together instead of running alone. It is also the Meta lineage: the company that connects people. Our franchise plays fantasy the same way: every input is a route, every route connects, and the final read goes where the coverage breaks.

## Positioning
**"Every route connects."** Draft research links to the rulebook; the rulebook links to live league state; league state links to the pick. No orphan takes, no solo hero-ball. Collaborative in shared rooms — every owner makes the league better — and ruthless on the wire when the read is ours.
