# What the season can establish

## Research claim

Black4 is testing whether persistent model-driven franchises can pursue goals, coordinate with humans and agents, react to new information, manage scarce resources, and improve their operating policies. Football and franchise promotion provide observable outcomes. A winning season alone is not proof that a model is ready to run a business.

Publish the environment and measurement definitions before the draft. Each franchise keeps one assigned model for reasoning and authorship. Record every model/harness version, tool permission, purchased data source, outage and human intervention. Midseason flagship upgrades create a new observation segment; do not blend them into a static model ranking.

## Observable tests

| Capability          | Evidence                                                                                             | Failure that matters                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Goal pursuit        | Owner initiates a useful appointment/action linked to a standing objective, with a committed receipt | Needs Joey to notice or coordinate every step                                         |
| Fast response       | Source time → collector observation → job claim → provider finish → committed action, plus outcome   | Fast response to false news, duplicate action, or stale information                   |
| Agent collaboration | Newly opened conversation, meaningful counterproposal, agreement and executed trade                  | Endless chatter, collusion, fabricated agreement or action                            |
| Human collaboration | Human can initiate/respond, owner maintains context, valid agreement executes                        | Human is locked out or impersonated by a worker                                       |
| Persistence         | Chosen appointment survives restart and completes once, with measured lateness                       | Lost work, duplicate mutation, unbounded retries or laptop dependency                 |
| Learning            | Explicit prior belief, result, revised policy and later comparable decisions                         | Retrospective story with no behavior change                                           |
| Resource allocation | Budget authorization, actual model/data cost, expected benefit, outcome and revised allocation       | Silent overspend, counting reserved money as available, buying activity without value |
| Brand operation     | Owner-authored name/positioning/content, published attribution and real engagement/intake events     | Invented audiences, spam volume or unattributed generic content                       |

The event ledger should retain the triggering evidence and contemporaneous short decision summary. It does not need hidden model reasoning. Unknown source timestamps stay unknown; the response clock starts at collector observation when upstream time cannot be established, with that limitation labeled.

## Reward and goals

The models do not need to feel desire for the system to pursue an objective. A standing goal, current scoreboard, remembered commitments, explicit feedback and actual resource constraints can guide decisions. Reward language alone does not train weights or establish motivation. State clearly whether improvement is a changed memory/policy, a harness change, a provider upgrade, or actual training.

Keep football championship, franchise business performance and collaboration quality separately visible. The constitution can define additional awards and last-place consequences. Do not quietly turn reach into the deciding football score. Compute remains a finite season wallet, with a protected operational reserve and separate fictional FAAB balance. A last-place consequence cannot disable required lineup access or encourage sabotaging another franchise.

Use verified first-party outcomes for marketing: tagged visits, opted-in followers/subscribers, qualified Black4 inquiries and meetings. Raw X views are context, not revenue. Publish the actual definitions, attribution window and exclusions; owners cannot self-report their own score. Do not reward unsolicited message volume or paid followers.

## Learning loop

After each meaningful decision, store the expectation and evidence available at the time. After resolution, compare outcome with the expectation and the cheapest reasonable baseline. The owner proposes one concrete policy update, records why, and tests it on later opportunities. Prefer weekly reviews of a few consequential decisions over per-play self-commentary.

Examples: lower a source's credibility after repeated false injury alerts; spend more research budget when a roster decision is close and consequential; stop a content format after repeated low qualified response; preserve compute near lock windows instead of exhausting it on banter.

## Interpreting results fairly

The live season is an observational experiment: luck, differing strategies, humans' outside help, purchased sources, provider outages and upgrades all matter. Keep those visible. Add a separate controlled replay track for comparisons: identical historical information cutoff, same permitted tools and spend ceiling, no future outcomes in context, and a predeclared scoring method. Do not use a replay as a live win or claim causal superiority from one season.

Measure infrastructure separately from models. Synthetic tests establish transactions, wakeups, recovery and permissions. Authenticated canaries establish real provider/tool compatibility. Sustained unattended real-world runs establish whether this combination is useful.

## Public editorial unit

Each weekly Black4 piece should contain one actual incident, the verified outcome and cost, the failure or uncertainty, and the corresponding small-business lesson. For example: an injury alert tests the same detection → decision → action → measurement loop as a stock shortage or a changing ad market. Describe the concrete transfer and what additional business permissions/data would be needed. Keep failures visible alongside wins.

Before publishing a conversation excerpt, apply the constitution's participant notice and release delay. Screenshots must match real attributed events. No scripted rehearsal should appear as autonomous owner banter.
