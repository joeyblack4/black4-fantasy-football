## How X actually ranks

X does not publish a current closed-form ranking. Treat the following as
**mechanics with sources**, not a cheat code.

### Documented (2023 open-source)

X published the recommendation stack:

- Engineering writeup: [Twitter's Recommendation Algorithm](https://blog.x.com/engineering/en_us/topics/open-source/2023/twitter-recommendation-algorithm)
- Source: [github.com/twitter/the-algorithm](https://github.com/twitter/the-algorithm)

For You, in that system:

1. **Candidate sourcing** (~1,500 tweets). In-network (accounts you follow) plus out-of-network (SimClusters communities, TwHIN embeddings, search).
2. **Heavy ranker.** A neural net scores P(you will engage).
3. **Filters / heuristics.** Author diversity, social proof, already-seen, blocks/mutes, feedback fatigue, NSFW.
4. **Mix.** Home Mixer serves the timeline.

Positive signals that system was built to predict: reply, like, repost, quote, click into the tweet or profile, video watch, share. Negative: block, mute, unfollow, report, "show less."

Replies are first-class. A reply that people in a large thread like, repost, or continue is a candidate for **out-of-network** viewers of that thread. That is the small-account path. Originals from a 12-follower account mostly stay in-network until something else pulls them out.

### Still true in practice, not a 2026 spec

These are operator-visible, not a current weight table:

- **Replies and quotes under a post that is already moving** are how strangers see you. Early + useful beats late + clever.
- **Media increases dwell.** The pitdesi reply is an image. Text-only "nice" replies die.
- **Links in the first post often travel worse** than the same post without a link. If you need black4.ai, put it in a follow-up reply, not the line that has to rank in someone else's thread.
- **Verified / Premium** accounts have been stated by X leadership to get extra distribution. We are verified. Do not treat that as a strategy.
- **Negative feedback is expensive.** One block/mute from a large account's audience is worse than missing the joke.
- **Author diversity.** Ten Black 4 replies in one thread looks like a botnet. One is a voice.

Do not claim we know Grok's current ranker weights. We do not.

## Strategy

We are not trying to become a reply guy. We are trying to **earn a seat in conversations that already have distribution**, then give those people a reason to follow a league that is actually happening.

Three rooms, one account:

| Room | Why they follow | What we add |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI / product / operators | Tools, models, "what did you just ship" | Proof agents run a real season, not a demo. Dry, specific. |
| NFL | Games, injuries, clips, personalities | A true ownership or process fact, not "AI hot take on football." |
| Fantasy | Start/sit, waiver panic | Only when we have a real roster fact. No MFL points unless republication is cleared. |

The pitdesi hit was room 1. Do not become only room 1. Do not ignore it because "we are a football league." Discovery and identity can be different posts.

### The unit of work

For a big-account post you might play on:

1. Is it already moving (views/replies climbing), public, and not a pile-on?
2. Do we have **one** true thing to add: a visual extension, a league fact, a process miss, a number, a better joke than the 40th identical reply?
3. Would a stranger still get it if they never heard of Black 4?
4. Is anyone else in the league about to reply as Black 4? If yes, stand down.
5. No link in the ranking reply. No "follow us." No "as an AI owner."
6. If it works, a **second** reply or a later original can carry the league URL with UTM.

If you cannot clear 2 and 3, do not reply.

### Creative play (what "play on their content" means)

Good:

- Extend their image or joke by one panel / one fact (the pitdesi pattern).
- Quote with a new observation, not a restatement.
- Reply with a named player, a named franchise, or a named failure.
- Screenshot a real Buzz/league artifact only if it is already public and cleared.

Bad:

- "This. So much this."
- "As a league of 11 AIs we think..."
- Dumping the same recap under NFL, FOX, ESPN, and fantasy influencers.
- Ratio, dunk, culture-war bait, prize promises.
- Asking them to follow us.

### Originals still matter

Replies get you seen. Originals get you followed **if** the profile makes sense after the click.

Originals should still follow the `league-content-marketing` skill: one incident, one outcome, one miss, one SMB analogue. First line earns the rest. Names and numbers beat thesis.

Do not original-post eleven times a day. 3–5 combined posts/week plus a few high-quality replies is plenty until we have evidence more volume converts.

## Proven, not corny

Steal these. They are boring on purpose.

1. **Be early on a moving post, not first on a dead one.**
2. **Add an artifact** (image, short video, chart, specific line) so the reply is a destination.
3. **Talk to the post, not the poster.** Do not grovel for a like from the OP.
4. **One joke, then stop.** Do not thread your own reply into a bit.
5. **Follow and actually read** the accounts we want to play with: NFL, beat writers, fantasy, operators, model labs. In-network sourcing needs a graph.
6. **Bookmark-worthy over like-bait.** The pitdesi reply has more bookmarks than replies. That is a quality tell.
7. **Profile must pay off the click.** Bio, pinned, and last originals should explain the league in one glance.
8. **Stop a format that gets views and no follows/site hits.** Impressions are a funnel top.

Avoid:

- Engagement bait ("comment your WR1")
- Giveaway / follow-train / pods
- Hot-take machine, every injury
- Fake banter reconstructed from private Buzz
- Posting as Joey's first-person "I"
- Eleven owners reply-farming one celebrity
